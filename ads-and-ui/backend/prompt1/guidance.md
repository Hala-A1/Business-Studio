# e& Brand Guidance

## Priority guidance for image generation
Apply these rules first — they have the most direct visual impact.

**Logo colourways (in order of preference):**
- Colourway A (preferred): white logo on e& Red (`#E00700`) background
- Colourway B (preferred): e& Red (`#E00700`) logo on white background
- Black logo: limited use only — fax or material-treatment contexts where red isn't possible
- The logo should appear in red or white in the vast majority of communications

**Brand colours:**
- e& Red: `#E00700` — primary brand colour, used prominently
- Ocean Blue: `#181F4B` — primary usage alongside e& Red
- Ink: `#14101F`
- White: used sparingly and only where necessary — typically against dark backgrounds, or when the layout is already predominantly red

**Clear space:**
- Keep the area around the logo free of other elements, text, or competing visuals
- When in doubt, give the logo more breathing room rather than less

**Banner/layout composition rules:**
- Vertical banners: full image background
- Horizontal banners: red background on the left holding all copy; image sits on the right
- Image cut-outs always sit on top of red backgrounds, never the reverse
- Smaller formats: always use a full red background
- End frames: always a full red background

**Tone:**
Bold, confident, customer-first. Reflects three core values: customer-obsessed (empathy, service-first), unite as one (connection, teamwork), dare to be bold (progress, disruption, forward motion).

## Full reference guidelines

### Clear space
Clear space is the area surrounding the logo that is kept free of other elements. Whenever possible, allow for more clear space than the minimum requirement. The minimum clear space is calculated in relation to the height of our symbol (the e& in the logo).

### Minimum size
- Vertical configuration: 36 pt minimum (measured to logo height)
- Horizontal configuration: 13 pt minimum

### Colour mode by medium
- RGB: digital use only — websites, social media, presentations, emails, videos
- CMYK: print use only — brochures, posters, business cards, physical event branding, packaging
- Never convert between RGB and CMYK manually; always use the provided artwork files

### Preferred colour versions
We prefer using the logo in white over e& Red backgrounds ("primary colourway A"), or in e& Red over white backgrounds ("primary colourway B"), wherever possible, to highlight the brand prominently. In the vast majority of communications, the logo appears in either red or white.

### Limited use
Use the black version only in limited instances where featuring the red version isn't possible, such as on a fax or material treatment. Request pre-approval from the Brand team before using these versions.

### Banner layout
- Vertical banners: full image background
- Horizontal banners: red background for all copy, positioned on the left; images sit on the right
- Image cut-outs always sit on top of red backgrounds
- Smaller formats always use a full red background
- End frames always have a full red background

### Online banner grid
- Split online banners into three equal parts to create a grid and a module X
- Module X is created from the margins
- Column gutter: 1/2 X
- Always split into two columns

### Core values icons (internal culture use only)
Used in this order:
1. **Be customer-obsessed** — outward radiating motif; everything starts from the customer and extends outward; represents empathy, attentiveness, service-first mindset
2. **Unite as one** — interlocking shape signifying strength through unity; connection, teamwork, collective momentum
3. **Dare to be bold** — sharp, upward-angled, forward-leaning mark; ambition, transformation, fearless innovation

**Icon colour usage:** primary is Ocean Blue and e& Red; secondary usage may feature e& Red alone. White may be used sparingly, typically against dark backgrounds or when the layout is already predominantly red.

**Icon usage restriction:** these icons are for internal communications initiatives only. Prior approval must be obtained from the Culture and People Comms team (People&) via people@e&.
